OUR APPROACH

Quality is a process.

Use these steps as a framework for discussing quality requirements. Specific certifications, laboratory facilities and test standards must be confirmed by the company.

01

Understand the brief

Agree product use, composition, dimensions, colour and finish expectations.

02

Check materials

Confirm material identification and incoming requirements for the order.

03

Inspect production

Use suitable checks at relevant stages based on product and customer needs.

04

Review finished goods

Assess final product details and packing against the agreed specification.

05

Document requirements

Keep approved samples, measurements and quality expectations clear.

06

Work together

Discuss issues early and align corrective actions with the customer.
