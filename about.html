About us

A family business
built on quality.

A family business built on quality, professional service and long-standing relationships. Our teams bring each stage of textile manufacturing together under one roof.

Company profile

Our vision and philosophy.

As a family business, our vision extends beyond financial quarters; we are driven by a generational responsibility to create value for our employees, customers, and community.

Our goal is to manufacture quality textile products with integrity, conduct ourselves in a professional and courteous manner, and exceed our customers' expectations through efficient service.

Our approach / CEO message

“Is this the way I would like my suppliers to deal with me?”

Our mission is to build a strong company through quality and diversified products, professional and courteous conduct, integrity, and responsive service to our customers.
